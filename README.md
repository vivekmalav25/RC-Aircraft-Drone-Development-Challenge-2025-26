# RC-Aircraft-Drone-Development-Challenge-2025-26

### SAE India Southern Section | Regular Class | AIR 19

**Duration:** Jul 2025 – Feb 2026  
**Role:** Design Lead  
**Team Size:** 6 Members

---

## Overview

Designed and developed a **fixed-wing RC aircraft** for the **SAE India Southern Section Drone Development Challenge 2025–26**.

As the **Design Lead**, I worked on the aircraft configuration, structural design, CAD modelling, component integration, fabrication, and flight testing. The design focused on achieving a balance between **aerodynamic performance, structural strength, stability, weight, and manufacturability**.

---

## Key Contributions

- Led the **aircraft design and development** as Design Lead.
- Designed and refined the **wing, fuselage, empennage, landing gear, and motor-mount structure**.
- Developed CAD models and design layouts for aircraft components and assembly.
- Worked on **structural layout, weight distribution, and center-of-gravity considerations**.
- Integrated major systems including **BLDC motor, ESC, battery, servos, receiver, control surfaces, and landing gear**.
- Designed the aircraft with a dedicated **payload/cargo bay** for competition requirements.
- Contributed to fabrication, assembly, component alignment, and design modifications.
- Performed ground and **flight testing** and refined the aircraft based on test observations.

---

## Aircraft Design

The aircraft consists of:

- Fixed-wing configuration
- Fuselage and lightweight structural frame
- Main wing with ailerons
- Vertical and horizontal empennage
- Elevator and rudder control surfaces
- BLDC propulsion system
- Servo-actuated control surfaces
- Landing gear
- Payload/cargo bay
- Radio receiver and battery system
